# RSI — the AI that improves AI

[![RSI: the AI that improves AI](guia/assets/banner.jpg)](https://inematds.github.io/rsi/guia/en/)

Fact-checked research on **recursive self-improvement (RSI)**, based on the September 2026 news (DeepSeek DSec, Anthropic's Opus 5.5 and "When AI builds itself", OpenAI's standards proposal). It covers what is real and what was exaggerated, how big tech, companies and governments will use it, and how business and technology people can benefit directly with **LOOP-R** (Execute → Measure → Critique → Propose → Test → Validate → Promote → Repeat).
